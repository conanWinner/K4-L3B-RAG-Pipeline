# Road to PGC: 2025 Point distribution

- **Source:** https://pubgesports.com/en/news/8264
- **Crawled:** 2026-09-25T04:28:21.502398+00:00

---

Hello, PUBG Esports fans!

As we gear up for the PUBG Esports 2025 season, we're excited to introduce the PGS & PGC point distributions for the upcoming global tournaments.

PUBG GLOBAL SERIES: Point matrix

The Road to PGC structure remains consistent in 2025, with four rounds of the PUBG Global Series, each offering crucial points. Additionally, the Esports World Cup (EWC) will once again be part of the PGS point system, adding extra challenges and rewards.

Only the top 8 teams with the highest accumulated points from the PGS Points race will secure a direct invitation to the PUBG Global Championship 2025.

PUBG GLOBAL SERIES: Seeds

The PUBG Global Series will once again feature 24 teams, comprising 14 teams from regional competitions and 10 PUBG Global Partner Teams.

PUBG Global Championship 2025: Seeds

The PUBG Global Championship 2025 will feature a total of 33 teams, an increase from previous tournament. Teams will qualify through regional series, the PUBG Global Series, and an additional Wild Card slot.

-

Wild Card grants a direct invitation to the PGC 2025 grand finals

-

If a team acquires a PGC seed with PGS points and acquires another slot at their regional series, the next team in rank at the team's regional series will be granted with a PGC seed.
