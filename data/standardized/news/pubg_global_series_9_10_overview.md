The Road to Glory Continues: PGS 9 and PGS 10 Arrives in Malaysia!

From the roaring cheers of past stages to the battles that shaped legends, PUBG Global Series has brought fans unforgettable moments.

Now, the journey continues—PGS 9 and PGS 10 lands in Malaysia this October, uniting 24 of global’s top teams for one last clash before PUBG Global Championship 2025 (PGC 2025).

-

When is it?

-

PGS 9: October 13 – October 19, 2025 (No matches on October 16 / Off-day)

-

PGS 10: October 27 – November 2, 2025 (No matches on October 30 / Off-day)

-

Where is it happening?

-

Seremban, Malaysia

-

Who’s competing?

-

24 of the best teams from all regions

-

What’s tournament format?

-

Tournament Structure

-

Group Stage: 24 teams divided into 3 groups (A/B/C). Each group will play a round-robin set of matches.

-

Day 1: Groups A & B

-

Day 2: Groups B & C

-

Day 3: Groups C & A

-

Final Stage

-

Based on Group Stage performance, 16 teams advance into Final Stage.

-

Final showdown across 3 days.

-

Match Format

-

Matches will be played across Erangel, Miramar, Taego, and Rondo.

-

Standard PUBG Esports ruleset S.U.P.E.R will apply.

-

Group Stage 12 matches per each group and Grand Finals 18 matches.

-

What’s on the line?

-

$300,000 prize pool and crucial PGS Points toward PGC 2025 qualification

-

Why should you watch?

-

It’s the final global series before PGC 2025—don’t miss the battles that set the stage for PUBG Global Championship.

Get ready for three weeks of intense action, new rivalries, and stories that will define the next chapter of PUBG Esports.
